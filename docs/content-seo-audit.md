### Sitewide findings (not unique to one URL)

- **Unexpanded Kayan shortcodes** on 1648 URLs (`[post_features]`, `[post_steps]`, `[post_prices]`, `[post_services]`, `[post_call]`). Those blocks never become unique HTML for Googlebot.
- **`tel:` call links remain in raw HTML** on 1648 posts (the live theme hides them with CSS; source still contains the CTA).
- **Shared Kayan article template** on 1509 URLs (boilerplate ratio typically 35–53% after city names are stripped).

The CSV still records these flags on every matching URL. Tables below highlight **URL-specific** risk: thin hubs, city-variant clones, and cross-service cannibalisation.

### How scores are calculated

- **Raw words**: whitespace tokens after scripts/styles/HTML are stripped.
- **Thin (raw)**: fewer than 1000 tokens — Google’s helpful-content bar for commercial service pages.
- **Unique words**: raw words minus sentences that appear in ≥8% of the corpus (Kayan template padding).
- **Template family**: identical H2 skeleton after city/service names are normalised.
- **Similarity**: 64-bit simhash on de-citied body tokens; Hamming ≤ 8 ≈ near-duplicate.
- **H1**: Kayan renders the post title as H1 in the theme. Body HTML without `<h1>` is expected and is **not** flagged.
- Unexpanded `[post_features]` / `[post_steps]` shortcodes mean those Kayan blocks never reach Googlebot as unique copy.

Most local-service URLs are **city variants of one Kayan article template**. Raw length is often 2,000+ words, but uniqueness is low. That is the core SEO risk: keyword cannibalisation across eight cities and weak distinctive value per URL.

## Recommendations

1. **Service CPT hubs** (`/services/{slug}/`): replace the 43-word stubs with unique 1,000+ word category guides that link down to city articles.
2. **City variants**: keep the URL map, but rewrite the first 300 words, FAQs, and proof (areas, climate, access) so eight cities are not the same article with a city token swapped.
3. **Expand or delete `[post_*]` shortcodes** so features/steps/prices exist as HTML, not leftover tokens.
4. **Remove `tel:` from post HTML** (hiding with CSS does not remove the node from the DOM or from some crawlers).
5. **Merge overlapping intents** (e.g. central AC vs duct maintenance in the same city) where Hamming ≤ 4.
6. **Utility pages** (FAQ, cities, contact, about): they can stay short, but add unique copy and do not compete with commercial articles.
